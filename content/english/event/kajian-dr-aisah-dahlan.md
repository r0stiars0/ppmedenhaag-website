### 👩‍⚕️ About the Speaker: dr. Aisah Dahlan, CMHt., CM.NLP.

dr. Aisah Dahlan is a medical doctor and preacher, known for her lectures on family life and *neuroparenting*. She studied medicine at Hasanuddin University (Unhas) in Makassar, then continued her medical professional programme at Gadjah Mada University (UGM) in Yogyakarta. She also holds certifications in hypnotherapy and *Neuro-Linguistic Programming* (NLP).

🧠 In her lectures, she explains how the brain works in simple language, linked to Islamic values, to help people understand communication, emotions, and relationships within the family.
