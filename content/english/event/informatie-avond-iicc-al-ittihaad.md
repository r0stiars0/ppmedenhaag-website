Assalamu'alaikum warohmatullahi wa barakatuh

Dear members and friends of PPME Den Haag,

We warmly invite you to an information evening about the plans to build the Indonesian Islamic Cultural Centre (IICC) Al Ittihaad. During this evening we will inform you about the plans, the developments and the future vision for our new Indonesian Islamic Cultural Centre.

It will be a great opportunity to look ahead together, exchange ideas and ask questions. We are building this project not only for ourselves, but also for our children, grandchildren and future generations.

Let us get to know this special initiative together, share ideas and contribute to realising our common goal. Your presence and involvement are greatly appreciated. Working together for our future!

**What can you expect?**

- Presentation of the new IICC Al Ittihaad mosque project
- Explanation of the plans and the current status
- Information on next steps and ways to contribute
- Opportunity to ask questions and think along

**Extra for our PPME members!** Attending PPME members who have not yet received PPME batik clothing will receive a PPME batik garment, while supplies last.
