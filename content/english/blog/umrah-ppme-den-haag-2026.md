Assalamu'alaykum warrohmatullohi wabarokatuh

Dear community members and friends of PPME Den Haag,

Alhamdulillahi Rabbil 'Alamin. The board of PPME Den Haag would like, in shaa Allah, to invite community members and friends of PPME Den Haag to perform Umrah in October.

This Umrah journey is one of PPME Den Haag's da'wah activities and is open to both members and non-members of PPME Den Haag.

The 'Umrah PPME Den Haag' was first organised in 2022 to mark the 50th anniversary of PPME Den Haag. Alhamdulillah, thanks to the success of the trip and the positive experiences of the participants, we have been guiding an Umrah group every year since.

In shaa Allah, this year we would like to organise an Umrah trip in October 2026, under the guidance of Ronald Kroon.

There are two departure dates: **8 October** and **16 October 2026**, both within the autumn school holiday (herfstvakantie).

For additional information, please contact **[Wati Chaeron](mailto:s.r.a.chaeron@gmail.com)**.

---

## 10-Day Umrah Package

**Price per person** (incl. visa application, flights, hotel in Makkah and Madinah incl. breakfast, transport, excursions, and guidance by an experienced guide (muttawwif)):

* € 1,995 for a 2-person room
* € 1,895 for a 3-person room
* € 1,795 for a 4-person room

If you would like to have dinner at the hotel, there is an additional charge of **€350 per person**.<br/>
Children (2–12 years) receive a **€125 discount**.

**Accommodation:**<br/>
Hotel in Makkah: Anjum<br/>
Hotel in Madinah: Al Manakha Rotana

*\*Prices are indicative.*
